Flash the ESP32-CAM

One time only. After this the camera streams to the website by itself, every time you power it on. It keeps the Wi-Fi it already saved.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Web Serial is not available in Safari or Firefox).
  2. Wire the USB-TTL adapter: 5V→5V, GND→GND, TX→U0RXD, RX→U0TXD, and connect GPIO0→GND.
  3. Press RST on the camera, then plug the adapter into the computer.
  4. Click Install and pick the serial port (COM…).
  5. When it finishes: disconnect GPIO0 from GND, press RST, and open the live page.

This browser cannot flash. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Flashing needs a secure (https) page — open this page from the live site.

Close the Arduino IDE / Serial Monitor first — an open port cannot be flashed. No libraries, no Wi-Fi password: the network saved on the camera is kept.

If the port list is empty or fails to connect: hold RST, click Install, and release RST the moment the port appears.

Already flashed? Just power the camera and open the live page — it connects on its own.

Successful flash — open Serial Monitor at 115200 and you should see [boot] MIRSAD camera v3 (HTTP RGB565), then [post] first frame accepted by the relay. If the first line still names v2 or MQTT, the flash did not take.
